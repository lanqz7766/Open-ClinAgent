# Radiology Report

## Clinical Indication
Brain MRI for surveillance of cognitive decline.

## Technique
MRI of the brain without and with contrast.

## Comparison
Compared with MRI brain dated 2024.

## Findings
Mild generalized cerebral volume loss is present. Hippocampal formations are symmetric in appearance on this exam. Mild scattered periventricular and subcortical white matter hyperintensities likely reflect chronic microvascular change. No acute infarct, intracranial hemorrhage, extra-axial collection, or midline shift.

## Impression
1. Mild generalized cerebral volume loss with mild chronic microvascular white matter change.
2. No acute intracranial abnormality.
